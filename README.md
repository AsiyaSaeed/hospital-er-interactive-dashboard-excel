# Hospital Emergency Room Operations Analysis Dashboard (using MS Excel)

## Project Objective
The hospital emergency room management wanted to analyze annual operational data for the emergency department. The goal was to help leadership and medical administrators understand patient flow, monitor wait times, evaluate department workloads, and improve overall emergency healthcare delivery across 2023 and 2024.

## Dataset used
- <a href="https://github.com/AsiyaSaeed/hospital-er-interactive-dashboard-excel/blob/main/Interactive%20excel%20dashboard.xlsx">Hospital dashbaord</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
* What is the total number of patients visiting the emergency room, and what are their monthly and yearly trends?
* What is the average wait time patients experience before seeing a medical professional?
* How do patient satisfaction scores trend across the operational period?
* How many patients were admitted versus not admitted?
* What is the patient age distribution across different brackets?
* What percentage of patients are seen on-time versus delayed?
* Who visits more often — Male or Female patients?
* Which medical departments receive the maximum patient referrals?

## Process
* Imported raw hospital data and performed data cleaning, filtering, and transformation using **Power Query**.
* Built a robust data model with calculated columns and measures using **DAX formulas**.
* Created pivot tables to aggregate metrics based on core business and operational questions.
* Designed comprehensive pivot charts and dynamic KPI summary cards.
* Combined all components into a single interactive dashboard layout equipped with Month and Year **slicers**.
---

## Project Insights
* **Patient Admission Split:** Roughly 49.78% of emergency room patients are admitted, while 50.22% are not admitted under the active filter selection.
* **Demographics & Gender:** Female patients account for 49% of visits, while male patients make up 51%.
* **Wait Times & Timeliness:** The average wait time stands at approximately 35.19 minutes, with 42% of attend statuses flagged as delayed versus 58% on-time.
* **Department Referrals:** General practice handles a significant portion of patient referrals, followed by orthopedics, while specialty fields like cardiology and gastroenterology receive lower immediate volumes.
* **Age Group Breakdown:** Patient volume spans evenly across age brackets, peaking slightly in the 10-19 and 50-69 age groups.

## Final Conclusion:
To improve emergency room operational efficiency and patient satisfaction scores (averaging 5.09), hospital management should implement targeted resource allocation during peak volume hours, particularly addressing bottlenecks causing delays in general practice and orthopedic referrals. Streamlining triage protocols for male and female patient demographics during high-traffic months will further reduce average wait times and enhance overall service delivery quality.
